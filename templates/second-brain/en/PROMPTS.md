# Ready-to-paste prompts

Open **Claude Code** (or Codex) in this folder and paste one. Each prompt is one paragraph: copy it whole.

## 1. Start: the AI interviews you and fills your second brain

```text
Read CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md and README.md in this folder: it is my second brain and you are going to maintain it. Interview me to fill it. Interview rules: ask me ONE question at a time and wait for my answer; start with my profile (name, how I want to be called, where I live, what I do, my goal this year, the tone I prefer and what you must never do) and fill the Owner profile section of AGENTS.md with it; then go through the areas in order (identity, health, people, work, money, projects, learning), 4 to 8 questions per area, going deeper when something matters; do not invent anything, mark what I did not tell you as pending; with my answers fill 01-identity/About Me.md and create the notes that are needed following the rules (front matter, Connections, area index, README.md); after each area show me a summary of what you wrote and ask if I want to correct it before moving on; when we finish, replace the template section of README.md (between the START HERE and END comments) with a short header with my name and a sentence that describes me, and write it all in Log.md. Start.
```

## 2. Everyday ingest

```text
Ingest: [paste or tell what happened]. Find the note where it fits and update it; if there is none, create one following the rules. Connect it with related notes, update the indexes and the log, and show me what you changed.
```

## 3. Ask your second brain

```text
Query: [your question]. Answer only with what is in my notes, citing them with links; if something is not there, tell me instead of filling in with general knowledge. If the answer is worth keeping, propose a new note.
```

## 4. Weekly maintenance

```text
Weekly maintenance: find broken links, notes nobody links to, contradictions between notes, ideas I mention often that have no note of their own, and data that changed. Show me the list before touching anything.
```
