## What this is

A folder of Markdown notes, one note per idea, inside 8 areas ordered by life priority. You talk; the AI writes, organizes and connects. It follows Andrej Karpathy's **LLM Wiki** pattern: the notes, the rules (`CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md`) and the navigation (this `README.md` as the index of everything, and `Log.md`, the record of each change).

**First step:** open Claude Code in this folder and paste prompt 1 from [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md). The AI interviews you, one question at a time, and fills your notes.

Ask Your Mentors can read this folder (read-only) to make your mentors' advice fit your life: turn on **Second brain** in its sidebar.
